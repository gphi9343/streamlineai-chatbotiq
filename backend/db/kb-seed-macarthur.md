Who is Macarthur Marble & Granite?

Macarthur Marble & Granite is a family owned and operated business with over
30 years experience. They supply and install benchtops, splashbacks, vanities,
fireplace surrounds, wall panelling, staircases and custom furniture, working
across mineral stone, natural stone and porcelain or sintered surfaces.

What materials and products do you offer?

Macarthur Marble & Granite work across three groups of surfaces:

- MINERAL STONE — the engineered-style surfaces (the current industry term is
  "mineral stone"). Brands include Caesarstone, Quantum Zero, YDL Stone, AC
  Stone, Vadara, Stone Ambassador Zenith, Talostone, Unistone and Cosentino
  Xeron.
- NATURAL STONE — stone like granite, marble and quartzite are examples of this
  category. Brands/suppliers include Cavart, CDK, WK, SMG, YDL Natural,
  Cosentino Sensa, SNB, Art of Marble and Avant Stone.
- PORCELAIN / SINTERED — examples include Smartstone, Dekton, YDL Porcelain,
  Slab HQ Sintered, Quantum Porcelain, Neolith, Talostone Sintered and Stone
  Ambassador Vasari. Porcelain is a strong option for OUTDOOR areas in
  particular.

They supply and install benchtops, splashbacks, vanities, fireplace surrounds,
wall panelling, staircases and custom furniture. The exact range of colours and
brands for a specific job is best discussed with the team.

How soon can you come and measure / install?

We book the install date in first, and then our measurer contacts you
approximately one week prior to that install date to organise the measure. We
lock the install in first and measure accordingly — it's not a case of the
sooner you measure, the sooner you install.

What do you need from me to give a quote?

For a quote, plans are helpful if you have them. If you don't have plans, that's
fine — a rough idea of your sizes is enough for the team to put a quote
together, and if you'd prefer, they can organise a measure for you instead.
A colour or even just a rough idea of the look you're after lets the team price
a few options to compare.
