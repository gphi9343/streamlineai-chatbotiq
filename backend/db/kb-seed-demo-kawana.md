What's the difference between hybrid, vinyl plank, laminate and engineered timber?

Each suits a different budget and situation:

- HYBRID combines the best of laminate and vinyl in a rigid board that floats
  above the slab. It's slightly thicker and softer underfoot — a durable
  all-rounder that suits most homes.
- VINYL PLANK is glued directly to the slab, 100% waterproof and quiet
  underfoot — perfect for wet areas and good value.
- LAMINATE gives a realistic timber look at a lower price point, but it's the
  least water-resistant of the group, so it's better suited to drier areas.
- ENGINEERED TIMBER is real timber over a stable ply core — the most natural
  look and feel and the most premium, with a bit more care needed around
  moisture.

Which one suits comes down to your budget, the rooms involved, and how much
natural timber character you're after. The team can talk you through it.

What's the best carpet for kids, pets and high-traffic areas?

For busy homes with kids and pets, a solution-dyed nylon loop-pile carpet is the
pick. Solution-dyed nylon has the colour locked right through the fibre, so it's
tough, stain-resistant and holds up to cleaning, and the loop-pile construction
wears well in high-traffic areas. It's the most practical choice when you need
carpet that lasts.

Is synthetic grass a good option in the Queensland climate?

Yes — synthetic grass is well suited to the Queensland climate. It stays green
through hot, dry stretches and heavy rain without watering, mowing or muddy
patches, which makes it a low-maintenance option for South East Queensland
yards, pet areas, and shady spots where real grass struggles.

How long does installation take?

It depends on the flooring and the size of the job. As a rough guide: carpet is
usually 1-2 days; a full-home vinyl plank install is around a week; direct-stick
timber can take up to 2 weeks. Most Sunshine Coast homes come together in about
2-3 days total. The team can give you a real timeframe once they've seen the job.

How soon can I walk on my new floor after it's installed?

Carpet and floating floors can be walked on straight away. Glue-down floors —
vinyl and direct-stick timber — need roughly 6 to 12 hours for the adhesive to
cure before you walk on them. The team will let you know the exact wait for your
floor on the day.

Can I install the flooring myself?

Some floors are DIY-friendly — hybrid and laminate can be a doable DIY job if
your subfloor is level and dry, since they float rather than glue down. Carpet
and direct-stick timber should always be installed professionally; the skill
involved is real, and professional installation is also a condition of the
warranty. If you're not sure whether your subfloor is up to it, the team can
advise.

How long will my new flooring last?

Properly installed and looked after, quality flooring typically lasts around 15
to 25 years. The actual life depends on the product you choose, the traffic it
gets, and how it's cared for.
